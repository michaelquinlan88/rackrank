# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 2025**

## Overview

RackRank ("we", "our", or "the App") is a clothing resale assistant that helps you create listings for online marketplaces. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect

### Photos
- Photos you capture are processed by AI to generate listing descriptions
- Photos are stored locally on your device
- Photos are only sent to our servers for AI processing

### Listing Data
- Titles, descriptions, and prices are stored locally on your device
- This data is not shared with third parties except when you choose to publish to a marketplace

### Account Data
- If you connect eBay or other marketplaces, their respective privacy policies apply
- We store authentication tokens locally to maintain your connection

## How We Use Your Data

- **AI Processing**: Photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate listing content
- **Marketplace Publishing**: When you publish, listing data is sent to your selected marketplace
- **No Advertising**: We do not sell your data or use it for advertising

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device
- We do not maintain user accounts or cloud storage
- Deleting the app removes all stored data

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Gemini AI**: For photo analysis and content generation
- **eBay API**: For publishing listings (when connected)

See their respective privacy policies for details.
